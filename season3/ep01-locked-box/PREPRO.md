# Ep01 — The Locked Box (pilot) · pre-production (brief steps 1–3)

## 1. Story breakdown
- **Hook (0–5 s):** close-up of a padlocked chest. "Three of the smartest men in the kingdom. One locked box. Only one got it open."
- **Build:** the king asks how you can tell if someone is truly intelligent. Scholar: knowledge. Inventor: speed. Shepherd: knowing when you're wrong. The king is unimpressed and sets the box challenge (a diamond inside, and the job of royal advisor as the prize).
- **Escalation:** the scholar picks the lock for hours (candles melt down). The inventor smashes it, and the hammer breaks first.
- **Twist:** the shepherd walks over and lifts the lid. The padlock stays dangling on the front: the box was never locked.
- **Moral:** the other two were so busy proving how clever they were that they never questioned the problem itself.
- **Loop:** the king slides a new locked box toward the camera, matching the hook frame.

## 3. Character sheets
**King Oswin** (68, the tester). Silhouette: wide pear (robe pools on the throne), crown on top. Crimson robe, ermine collar and hem, gold crown with red and teal gems, big cloud-white beard, heavy white brows. Personality: dry, skeptical, secretly playful. Posture: leans back on the armrests, one brow up.

**Corvin** (52, the scholar). Silhouette: tall narrow column with a tall hat (tallest shape on screen). Navy robe with gold trim and stars, tall navy hat with gold band and tassel, round spectacles, long nose, grey goatee, red book under his arm. Personality: smug lecturer. Posture: chin up, finger raised.

**Bram** (45, the inventor). Silhouette: short, wide barrel. Mustard shirt, brown leather apron with a wrench pocket, brass goggles on his forehead, auburn moustache and side tufts, hammer on his shoulder. Personality: impatient, brute-force optimist. Posture: fist on hip, feet planted wide.

**Wren** (27, the shepherd). Silhouette: slim and plain, with a shepherd's crook rising above him (a readable shape at any size). Sage tunic, rope belt, patched brown cloak, messy straw hair. Personality: calm, curious, unbothered. Posture: relaxed, slightly half-lidded eyes, small smile.

**Guards** (background). Teal tabards, silver helmets with red plumes, spears. Stiff idle sway.
