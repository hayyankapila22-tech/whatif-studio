# LF02 sources (fact-checked 2026-11-21)
10 Singapore gum — sale/import banned since 3 Jan 1992; chewing legal; sellers fined up to S$2,000; 2004 therapeutic/dental/nicotine gum via pharmacist/dentist; caning = myth.
   https://sso.agc.gov.sg/SL/272A-RG4 · https://www.nbcnews.com/id/wbna5066384 · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chewing_gum_sales_ban_in_Singapore
9 Canada coins — Currency Act s.8(2): $40 in $2+ coins, $25 in $1, $10 in 10c–<$1, $5 in 5c, 25c in 1c; s.8(3) same-day payments aggregated; no penalty, creditor may refuse.
   https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-52/section-8.html
8 Germany — StVO §18(8) no stopping on Autobahn incl. hard shoulder; empty tank treated as avoidable; BKatV €30 stopping / €70 parking (ADAC: up to €70 + point); spare can doesn't help (ADAC).
   https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__18.html · https://www.adac.de/verkehr/tanken-kraftstoff-antrieb/tipps-zum-tanken/tankanzeige/
7 Barcelona — 2011 civic ordinance (swimwear/shirtless €120–300); since 15 Feb 2026 coexistence by-law: bare torso €120–300, sport exempt; beaches/promenade exempt; police ask first.
   https://www.catalannews.com/society-science/item/barcelona-city-council-bans-nudity-and-semi-nudity-on-the-streets · https://es.ara.cat/sociedad/barcelona/claves-nueva-ordenanza-convivencia-barcelona_1_5649264.html
6 Venice — St Mark's feeding ban 2008; ~40,000 pigeons, ~1/3 through St Mark's daily; seed sellers closed; today art. 24 municipal rules: pigeons or seagulls, €25–500.
   https://comune.venezia.it/en/node/7156 · https://www.nbcnews.com/id/wbna24390018
5 Rome — Urban Police Regulation 2019: no sitting on Spanish Steps (135 steps), €250, €400 if dirtied/damaged; also restricts visible washing.
   https://ansa.it/english/news/2019/08/06/sitting-on-romes-spanish-steps-banned_6d9d8906-4ed8-478e-9ea3-fd46f956eeb4.html
4 USA — 18 U.S.C. § 706: red Greek cross on white / "Red Cross" / "Geneva Cross" reserved to American Red Cross + military medical services; fine and/or up to 6 months; pre-1948 uses exempt. (Smokey Bear § 711 was repealed in 2020 — not used.)
   https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/706
3 Switzerland — Animal Protection Ordinance (TSchV) 2008, Art. 13 social contact + Annex 2: guinea pigs at least in pairs.
   https://www.admin.ch/opc/de/classified-compilation/20080796/202007140000/455.1.pdf
2 UK — Licensing Act 1872 s.12 (England & Wales): drunk in charge of carriage, horse, cattle, steam engine, or with loaded firearm; level 1 fine (£200) or up to 1 month; motor vehicles excluded (RTOA 1988).
   https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/Vict/35-36/94/section/12
1 UK — Salmon Act 1986 s.32 "Handling salmon in suspicious circumstances"; now incl. trout, eels, lampreys, smelt, freshwater fish; defence if no offence committed; unlimited fine on indictment.
   https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1986/62/section/32
Bonus — Statute Forbidding Bearing of Armour 1313 (England & Wales), still on legislation.gov.uk, not repealed.
