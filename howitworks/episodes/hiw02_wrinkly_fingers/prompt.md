## 0. WHAT THIS EPISODE IS

- **Format:** "HOW DOES IT WORK?" explainer #2, the same premium "lab" look as the blackheads episode.
- **Jeremy:** **not in this episode.** The scale references are a glass marble, a car tyre and the viewer's own hand. Keep a slot for a Jeremy cameo in a future S1-J variant: Jeremy in a bathtub, looking at his fingers.
- **Topic:** Why fingertips prune in water. The real answer is nerves and blood vessels, not water soaking in. Then: what the wrinkles might be for, and the honest scientific argument about it.
- **Topic score:** curiosity 5 · visual potential 5 · sourceable facts 5 = **15/15**.
- **Template:** body explainer (myth-bust → mechanism → proof → "well, not exactly" science-fight twist).
- **Why it'll perform:** everyone has seen it, almost everyone believes the wrong reason, and the episode ends on a genuine open question (perfect comment bait).
- **Tone:** curious, playful, honest about uncertainty. **Never overclaim**: the grip benefit is presented as debated, because it is.

## 3. FULL SCRIPT (control: `H4-S1-C1-P2-E1-L60`)

Markup follows master prompt §3. **Spoken word count: 154 → about 55 s with pauses.**

```
Your fingers *wrinkle* in the bath.
But it's not *water* || soaking in. //
If it were, || your *arms* would wrinkle too.
It's your *nerves*. //
After about *five minutes*, || your fingertips *notice* the water.
They send a signal || to your *brain*. //
And your brain does something *strange*.
It squeezes the *blood vessels* || under your skin.
The fingertip *shrinks* a little... //
but the skin on top || stays the *same size*.
So it *buckles* || into wrinkles. {SFX:hit}
The proof? || If the nerve to a finger is *damaged*, || that finger *won't* wrinkle. //
{CHAPTER}
So... || what are they *for*? //
In 2013, || scientists tested it with wet *marbles*.
Wrinkly fingers moved them || *12%* faster.
Like the *treads* || on a tyre. //
Well... || *not exactly*. {SFX:hit}
A year later, || another team *repeated* it... //
and found *no difference*.
Scientists are still *arguing*. //
{CHAPTER}
But one study found something cool: || your wrinkles form the *same pattern*, || every *time*.
So next time your fingers wrinkle in the bath...
```
