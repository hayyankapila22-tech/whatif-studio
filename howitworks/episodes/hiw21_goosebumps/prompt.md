## Facts (fact-checked 2026-11-23)
- Live Science "Why do we get goosebumps?" (2022): one tiny muscle for each hair; raises the hair = goosebump; cold, scared, strong emotions; fluffs fur into insulation and makes furry animals look larger when threatened; vestigial in humans.
- Popular Science "The evolutionary mystery of goosebumps" (2018): muscle at each hair root contracts, hair stands up; cold, fear, music; fight-or-flight / adrenaline; traps air near the skin.
