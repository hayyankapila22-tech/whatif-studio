## Facts (fact-checked 2026-11-21) — Mayo Clinic "Eye floaters" (2024)
- Floaters = clumps of collagen fibres in the vitreous (jelly) casting shadows on the retina; mostly age-related.
- Move when your eyes move, dart away when you try to look at them; most visible against blue sky / white wall.
- Many people over 50 have them.
- Emergency signs: many more / sudden new floaters, flashes of light, a grey curtain or dark area in your vision.
