## Facts (fact-checked 2026-11-22)
- Britannica "Why Do Sliced Apples Turn Brown?": cutting exposes tissue to oxygen; enzyme polyphenol oxidase turns polyphenols brown (melanins); lemon/pineapple juice acids slow it; refrigerate; pears, bananas brown too.
- Scientific American "Why do cut apples turn brown?": enzyme kept in the cell's plastids, acts once the cell breaks and oxygen enters; lower pH (acid) makes the enzyme less active.
- UW-Madison "Curiosities: Why do apple slices turn brown?": breaking the cells exposes the enzyme to polyphenols and oxygen; pigments are melanins; avocados and potatoes too.
