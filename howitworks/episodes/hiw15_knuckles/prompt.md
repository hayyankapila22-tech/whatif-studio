## Facts (fact-checked 2026-11-21)
- Pulling the joint apart lowers pressure in the joint fluid → gas bubble → the pop — Harvard Health.
- Where exactly the sound comes from (bubble forming vs collapsing) has been debated — Harvard Health blog (2018). The video
  only says "a gas bubble... that's the crack", which is true under both views.
- Roughly 20–30 minutes before the same joint can crack again (gas re-dissolves) — Scientific American, HowStuffWorks.
- Probably harmless; no higher arthritis risk in habitual crackers — Harvard Health. Pain/swelling → stop / get checked.
