## Script
How does a mosquito *find* you in the dark? / Step one: your *breath*! / You breathe out *carbon dioxide*, and they can
sense it from about *15 metres* away. / Step two: your *smell*. / Tiny germs on your skin make a scent they *love*. That's

## Facts (fact-checked 2026-11-23)
- Houston Methodist (2022): CO2 sensed "upwards of 50 feet" (~15 m); skin-bacteria odours attract them and explain why some people get bitten more; heat is the close-range cue; females need blood nutrients to make eggs.
- HealthDay/Drugs.com (2024, Nature paper): heat works up to ~2.5 ft; poor vision; loose clothing blocks bites and interferes with heat detection.
- ASU Ask A Biologist "Mosquito magnet": females need blood to develop eggs; loose thick clothing.
