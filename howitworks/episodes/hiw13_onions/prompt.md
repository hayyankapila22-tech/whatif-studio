## Facts (fact-checked 2026-11-20)
- Cutting ruptures cells; an enzyme meets sulfur compounds normally kept apart, producing a gas (syn-propanethial-S-oxide);
  nerves in the eye detect it and tear glands flush it away — Mental Floss; All About Vision.
- More cutting/crushing = more irritant; a sharp knife crushes fewer cells; ~30 min in the freezer cut the fumes; leave the
  root end for last — All About Vision. Temporary irritation only.
