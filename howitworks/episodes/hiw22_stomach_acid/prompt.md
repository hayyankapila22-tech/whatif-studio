## Facts (fact-checked 2026-11-23)
- Live Science "Why doesn't stomach acid burn through our stomachs?" (2026): acid strong enough to dissolve metal; thick sticky alkaline mucus rich in bicarbonate buffers it, continually renewed; breaks let acid in -> ulcers; NSAID painkillers and H. pylori bacteria weaken the mucus.
- Univ. of Alaska Geophysical Institute "Why doesn't the stomach digest itself?": zinc dissolves in gastric acid; lining replaces itself in about three days (script says "every few days").
