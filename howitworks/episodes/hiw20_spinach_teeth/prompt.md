## Facts (fact-checked 2026-11-22)
- Live Science (2016) "Why does spinach make your teeth feel weird?": oxalic acid + calcium → calcium oxalate crystals that don't dissolve well in water; lemon juice reduces the film; spinach has more oxalic acid than other crops.
- BBC Science Focus: calcium in saliva + oxalic acid → insoluble crystals that stick to teeth; spinach does not damage your teeth; milk's calcium makes it worse.
- America's Test Kitchen "Spinach and chalky residue": oxalic acid released when chewing combines with calcium in saliva; exaggerated with milk and cheese.
