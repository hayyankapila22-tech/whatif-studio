## Facts (fact-checked 2026-11-20)
- The brain predicts the feeling of touch caused by your own movement and uses that prediction to cancel/dampen it; it can't
  predict someone else's touch — Scientific American (Blakemore), "Why can't a person tickle himself?".
- The prediction is made from a copy of the movement command; it helps the brain separate self-made sensations from outside
  ones — Mental Floss, ScienceAlert.
